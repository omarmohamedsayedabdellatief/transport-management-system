# ON TIME brand

The custom OT monogram combines a continuous transport route, a junction/clock-hand shape and an amber destination. It was generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 26 September 2026 and inspected before integration. It replaces the generic bus brand icon in the sidebar, header, sign-in screen and favicon. The original generated PNG is retained unchanged, including transparency.

Asset: `client/public/brand/on-time-mark.png` (1280 × 1280 PNG).

Palette: deep petrol `#102E2A`, primary teal `#087F75`, soft mint `#C5EFDA`, amber `#F1B44C`, warm canvas `#F5F6F2`, ink `#193B37`. Red, amber and green remain meaningful operational states. Lemonada is retained for both languages.

## Generation prompt

Create a professional original logo mark for ON TIME, an Egyptian corporate employee transportation management platform. Deliver ONE square PNG logo asset on a transparent background, not a mockup. The symbol is a distinctive interwoven O and T monogram: the O forms a continuous rounded transport route, the T is subtly a road junction / precise clock hand, with one small amber destination terminal. Geometric elegant flat vector-like design, compact bold silhouette, recognizable at 32px, excellent optical balance. Rich deep teal #087F75, bright turquoise #53D6BD and warm amber #F1B44C, with sufficient contrast on white or dark petrol surfaces. No generic bus, car, location-pin, stock icon or literal clock face. No text, letters outside the abstract monogram, shadows, gradients, textures, borders, captions or presentation board. A single centered mark occupying about 85% of the canvas, transparent corners and transparent negative spaces. This will be the authentic custom brand mark used in the navigation, login and favicon.
